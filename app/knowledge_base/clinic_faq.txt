DEMO CLINIC INFORMATION

Clinic Timings:
The demo clinic is open Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 6 PM.

Patients should request an appointment before visiting.

Doctor schedules may change. Users should contact clinic reception to confirm doctor availability.

Emergency Policy:
For a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.
